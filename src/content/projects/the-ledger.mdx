The Ledger is an agentic event store and enterprise audit infrastructure. It acts as the immutable memory and governance backbone for multi-agent AI deployments — every agent decision, tool call, and state transition is recorded as a tamper-evident event, queryable across the lifetime of a workflow.

Designed to bring production-grade traceability to AI automation: when a multi-agent system takes an action, you can replay exactly how it got there, who (which agent) authored each step, and what evidence shaped the decision.
