Chronos is a next-gen, AI-powered financial super app redefining personal finance through biometric-first security and intelligent automation. The platform acts as a smart financial assistant — delivering personalized insights, budgeting strategies, and investment recommendations derived from real transaction data.

As the founding AI Engineer, I designed and built the AI advisor, deployed automated investment strategies across crypto, stocks, and NFTs, and architected the multi-currency account infrastructure that powers transfers, exchanges, and portfolio management.
